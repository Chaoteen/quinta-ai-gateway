# About Quinta AI Gateway

Quinta AI Gateway helps enterprises and developers connect AI models and manage their usage. A unified gateway connects internal agents, business applications and developer tools while bringing access permissions, quota and cost records together.

## AI access and everyday operations

Enterprises can select models for their applications, create separate API keys and review usage and billing records. Individual developers can also register and start integrating once they have quota and model access.

Our primary audience is enterprises using AI directly. We continue to develop subscriptions, quota replenishment and operational workflows around model APIs and enterprise agents. Available models, pricing and service scope depend on configuration and the agreed contract.

## Our priorities

- **Convenient integration** through a unified gateway rather than separate integrations with every provider.
- **Clear access boundaries** through roles, application keys and access restrictions.
- **Reviewable costs** through usage, billing and invoice request pages.
- **Practical delivery** of subscriptions, vouchers, promotions and agent services as capabilities become available.

## Get started

Read the [API access guide](__API_GUIDE_URL__) to create a key, discover models and send a first request, or explore the [model catalog](__MODELS_URL__).

New personal accounts start with zero balance. Contact your existing platform representative for enterprise onboarding, resources, trial quota and contracted services. A general support contact has not yet been published.

## Service status

This website is a preview under active development. Provider branding does not mean every model is enabled. Undelivered capabilities such as an agent marketplace, knowledge base and workflows are marked as coming soon. Real online payments and automatic invoicing are not enabled; invoice requests require finance review and handling for the actual transaction. Preview content is not a certification or service-level guarantee.
